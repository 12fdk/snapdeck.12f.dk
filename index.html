FAQ

Fair questions

Is it really free?
Yes — three new decks a month, and everything else (studying, streaks, stats, offline use) is included. Premium is a one-time purchase that removes the deck, document length and card limits. No subscription, no trial that quietly charges you.
Does it work offline, in a lecture hall with no wifi?
That's the whole idea. The AI runs on your iPhone, so making decks and studying both work in a basement seminar room, on a train, or in airplane mode.
Can you see my notes?
No. There's no account and nothing is uploaded — your photos, PDFs and decks stay on your device. Details are in the privacy policy.
What subjects and languages does it handle?
Anything text-based: biology, history, law, medicine, computer science, vocabulary, and so on. The app itself is available in 39 languages, and it builds decks from material in the language you're studying.
Will it read my handwriting?
Usually. Clear handwriting in decent light works well; a rushed page photographed in a dark room is harder. You can edit any card afterwards, or type the text in yourself.
Which iPhones does it need?
An iPhone with Apple Intelligence — iPhone 15 Pro or later, running iOS 26. That's what makes the on-device AI possible.
